# AIGGPA Research Project: Glossary & Keywords Dictionary

This dictionary defines key academic frameworks, statistical measures, and state/national e-governance platforms utilized in the evaluation of digital tool adoption across government departments in Madhya Pradesh.

---

## 1. Academic & Theoretical Frameworks

### Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)
* **Definition:** An information systems theory developed by Fred Davis (1989) that models how users come to accept and use a technology. It posits that system use is determined by behavioral intention, which is driven by two main beliefs: Perceived Usefulness and Perceived Ease of Use.

### Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT)
* **Definition:** A consolidated framework formulated by Venkatesh et al. (2003) that explains user intentions to use an information system and subsequent usage behavior. It groups factors into four core constructs: Performance Expectancy, Effort Expectancy, Social Influence, and Facilitating Conditions.

### Perceived Usefulness (PU) / Performance Expectancy (PE)
* **Definition:** The degree to which an employee believes that using a specific digital tool will help them perform their job duties more efficiently, improve output quality, or increase productivity.
* **Project Variables:** Measured via Q11 (Faster than paper), Q12 (Improves quality), Q13 (Increases productivity), and Q14 (Suited to job).

### Effort Expectancy (EE)
* **Definition:** The degree of ease associated with the use of the system.
* **Project Variables:** Measured via Q15 (Difficulty level, reversed), Q16 (Confidence in usage), and Q29 (User interface friendliness).

### Social Influence (SI)
* **Definition:** The degree to which an individual perceives that important others (superiors, peers, or colleagues) believe they should use the new system.
* **Project Variables:** Measured via Q17 (Superior encouragement) and Q18 (Colleague usage).

### Facilitating Conditions (FC)
* **Definition:** The degree to which an individual believes that an organizational and technical infrastructure exists to support use of the system.
* **Project Variables:** Measured via Q22 (Internet/connectivity quality) and Q24 (Access to IT helpdesk support).

## 2. Statistical Metrics

### Cronbach's Alpha ($\alpha$)
* **Definition:** A measure of internal consistency or reliability of a set of items, questions, or ratings. It evaluates how closely related a set of items are as a group. A score of $\ge 0.70$ is the standard threshold indicating acceptable scale reliability.

### Mann-Whitney U Test
* **Definition:** A non-parametric statistical test used to compare differences between two independent groups on an ordinal or continuous variable (e.g., comparing Likert scores between Head Office vs. District Office staff).

### Kruskal-Wallis H Test
* **Definition:** A non-parametric method for testing whether multiple independent groups originate from the same distribution (e.g., comparing Likert sentiment scores across four different departments).

## 3. National-Level E-Governance Platforms

### e-HRMS 2.0 (Electronic Human Resource Management System)
* **Definition:** A unified digital platform developed by the NIC and managed by DoPT for central government employees. It digitizes personnel records, tour management, leave, LTC, loans, and e-Service Books.

### iGOT Karmayogi (Mission Karmayogi)
* **Definition:** An online capacity-building portal designed to transition public officials from a rule-based system to a role-based competency model. It hosts competency-mapped training modules across multiple languages.

### e-Office
* **Definition:** A digital workplace solution developed by NIC to eliminate physical file movement in government offices, replacing paper processing with digital workflows (eFile and eReceipt).

### SPARROW (Smart Performance Appraisal Report Recording Online Window)
* **Definition:** A web-based application that digitizes the submission, routing, and assessment of Annual Performance Appraisal Reports (APARs) for government officers.

### PFMS (Public Financial Management System)
* **Definition:** A web-based database system developed by the Controller General of Accounts (CGA) to monitor programs, track public funds, and manage Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) and payroll.

### GeM (Government e-Marketplace)
* **Definition:** A dedicated online portal for the procurement of common-use goods and services required by government departments, public sector undertakings, and autonomous bodies.

## 4. Madhya Pradesh State-Specific Portals

### MPOnline
* **Definition:** A collaborative portal between the Government of Madhya Pradesh and TCS that serves as a centralized gateway for citizen-centric services, university registrations, and recruitment.

### MP Bhulekh / WebGIS
* **Definition:** The digital land records portal of Madhya Pradesh used by Patwaris, Revenue Inspectors, and citizens to access land maps (Khasra/Khatauni) and manage mutations.

### RCMS (Revenue Case Management System)
* **Definition:** An online application that tracks and manages revenue disputes and cases in Madhya Pradesh courts, allowing orders to be processed and uploaded digitally.

### SAMPADA 2.0
* **Definition:** The state portal for digitized property registration, stamp duty calculations, and e-stamping, integrating property records with revenue databases.

### NMMS (National Mobile Monitoring System)
* **Definition:** A mobile application deployed by the Ministry of Rural Development to record twice-daily geotagged attendance of workers at MGNREGA worksites to ensure transparency.

### ANMOL MP (ANM Online)
* **Definition:** A tablet-based application used by Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs) in Madhya Pradesh to digitize maternal and child health tracking, vaccine logs, and demographic data.

### eVIN (Electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network)
* **Definition:** A digital cold chain monitoring system that provides real-time information on vaccine stocks, logistics, and storage temperatures at healthcare facilities.

### MAP_IT (Madhya Pradesh Agency for Promotion of Information Technology)
* **Definition:** The nodal agency responsible for implementing e-governance policies, IT infrastructure, and state-wide networks (like SWAN) in Madhya Pradesh.
