Core Research Insights & Bottlenecks

Forest Department Survey Findings

Administrative Footprint

e-Office, Government Email, and MS Office form the core administrative base, showing 75% adoption. Collaboration platforms (Drive, Zoom, Docs) sit at 25%.

The Infrastructure Barrier

Slow internet connectivity, power outages, and complex portal authentication represent the largest daily work disruptions cited by both office and field guards.

Targeted Skills Mismatch

While 57.5% received generic IT training, 42.5% received none. High demand exists for role-specific field training on GIS maps, GPS tracking, and e-Green Watch.
